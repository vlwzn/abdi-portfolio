Every project is scoped individually. Once the brief, deliverables, and timeline are clear, I’ll share a tailored proposal before any work begins.

Yes. I’m based in Indonesia and available worldwide. Clear communication, scheduled check-ins, and shared feedback keep remote collaboration simple.

Absolutely. Design, motion, and editing can be brought together in one project to create a consistent visual story across your touchpoints.
